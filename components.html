A BEGINNER'S FIELD GUIDE

01 / UNDERSTAND THE HARDWARE

Every part.
A purpose.

A PC is a team of components. Here's what each one brings to the build—and what to check before it goes in your cart.

CPUGPUMotherboardRAMStoragePower supplyCoolingCase
01 / HARDWARE

The processor

CPU

Runs program instructions and handles general computing tasks. Think of it as the part that carries out the work your software asks for.

Before you buy

Check the motherboard socket, chipset support, and required BIOS version.

02 / HARDWARE

The graphics card

GPU

Renders graphics for games and visual workloads. A dedicated graphics card is separate from the integrated graphics found in some processors.

Before you buy

Check case clearance, power requirements, and the connectors your power supply provides.

03 / HARDWARE

The connection point

Motherboard

Connects your processor, memory, storage, expansion cards, and ports so the system can work together.

Before you buy

Match the CPU socket, memory type, and board size to the rest of your build.

04 / HARDWARE

The working memory

RAM

Holds information that programs are actively using. It is temporary memory, unlike the long-term files saved on a storage drive.

Before you buy

DDR4 and DDR5 are not interchangeable. Follow the board manual for supported kits and slots.

05 / HARDWARE

A home for your files

Storage

Stores your operating system, games, applications, and documents. SSDs have no moving parts and are common in modern desktops.

Before you buy

Check the drive interface and size. An M.2-shaped drive must also match the slot’s supported protocol.

06 / HARDWARE

Power for every part

Power supply

Converts wall power into the electrical power used by the PC. Its capacity and available cables need to suit the whole system.

Before you buy

Use the cables supplied with your exact PSU, or cables explicitly approved for that model.

07 / HARDWARE

Keep temperatures in check

Cooling

A CPU cooler moves heat away from the processor. Case fans help exchange warm air inside the case with cooler outside air.

Before you buy

Check socket mounting support, cooler height or radiator clearance, and fan connections.

08 / HARDWARE

The frame of your build

Case

Holds and protects the components. Layout affects available space, airflow, cable routing, and how easy the PC is to assemble.

Before you buy

Confirm motherboard size, GPU length, cooler clearance, and power supply fit.

THE MOST IMPORTANT CHECK

Compatibility comes first.

A great component is only useful if it works with the rest of your system. Use the exact model's manufacturer specifications and manuals as your final reference.
